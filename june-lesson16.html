June 16:
Around Town and Where Things Are

Words 226 to 250, little words, the places you go, and how to say where something is

Lesson Goals

By the end of this lesson…

  • 1Soften a sentence or make a suggestion with 吧
  • 2Make people plural with 们, and join two nouns with 和
  • 3Name the places you go: 学校, 医院, 商店, 超市, 公司
  • 4Tell the school words apart: 大学, 中学, 小学
  • 5Say where something is with 上, 下, 里, 外
  • 6Use 中国 and the word 国 for country

Little Words

吧, 们, 和.

吧, suggestion or softener
我们走吧。
Wǒmen zǒu ba.
Let us go.
们, plural for people
学生们都来了。
Xuéshengmen dōu lái le.
The students have all come.
和, joins two nouns
我和老师在学校。
Wǒ hé lǎoshī zài xuéxiào.
The teacher and I are at school.

吧 makes a sentence a gentle suggestion. 们 turns 学生 into 学生们 (students). 和 links two nouns the way "and" does.

Places You Go

Where are you going?

我去学校。
Wǒ qù xuéxiào.
I go to school.
妈妈在医院。
Māma zài yīyuàn.
Mom is at the hospital.
我们去超市买东西。
Wǒmen qù chāoshì mǎi dōngxi.
We go to the supermarket to buy things.

Pair these places with 去 (go) and 在 (be at), the verbs from last week. 商店, 超市, 书店, 饭店, and 公司 all work the same way.

Schools

大学, 中学, 小学.

小学生在小学。
Xiǎoxuéshēng zài xiǎoxué.
Primary schoolers are at primary school.
中学生在中学。
Zhōngxuéshēng zài zhōngxué.
Middle schoolers are at middle school.
我在大学学习。
Wǒ zài dàxué xuéxí.
I study at university.

小学 is primary, 中学 is middle, 大学 is university. The student words match: 小学生 and 中学生.

Where Things Are

上, 下, 里, 外.

thing, then place, then position word
书在桌子上。
Shū zài zhuōzi shàng.
The book is on the table.
猫在房间里。
Māo zài fángjiān lǐ.
The cat is in the room.
学校在前，医院在后。
Xuéxiào zài qián, yīyuàn zài hòu.
The school is in front, the hospital is behind.

The position word comes last: 桌子上 (on the table), 房间里 (in the room). 上 up, 下 down, 里 inside, 外 outside, 前 front, 后 back.

Putting It Together

A few places.

学校里有很多学生。
Xuéxiào lǐ yǒu hěn duō xuésheng.
There are many students in the school.
我和朋友去超市。
Wǒ hé péngyou qù chāoshì.
My friend and I go to the supermarket.
东西都在房间里。
Dōngxi dōu zài fángjiān lǐ.
The things are all in the room.
中国有很多大学。
Zhōngguó yǒu hěn duō dàxué.
China has many universities.
