June 17:
Food, Meals, and How You Feel

Words 251 to 275, food on the table, the three meals, feeling unwell, and the weather

Food on the Table

吃饭, 喝水.

我吃米饭和菜。
Wǒ chī mǐfàn hé cài.
I eat rice and vegetables.
我喜欢吃饺子和包子。
Wǒ xǐhuan chī jiǎozi hé bāozi.
I like to eat dumplings and steamed buns.
早上我吃面包，喝牛奶。
Zǎoshang wǒ chī miànbāo, hē niúnǎi.
In the morning I eat bread and drink milk.

Use 吃 for food and 喝 for drinks. 和 joins them: 米饭和菜.

The Three Meals

早饭, 午饭, 晚饭.

我早上吃早饭。
Wǒ zǎoshang chī zǎofàn.
I eat breakfast in the morning.
中午我和同学吃午饭。
Zhōngwǔ wǒ hé tóngxué chī wǔfàn.
At noon I eat lunch with a classmate.
晚饭我吃面条儿。
Wǎnfàn wǒ chī miàntiáor.
For dinner I eat noodles.

早, 午, and 晚 plus 饭 give the three meals. The time word can lead: 早上吃早饭.

Feeling Sick

生病, 看病.

我今天生病了。
Wǒ jīntiān shēngbìng le.
I got sick today.
我去医院看病。
Wǒ qù yīyuàn kànbìng.
I go to the hospital to see a doctor.
我有一个问题。
Wǒ yǒu yí gè wèntí.
I have a question.

生病 is to get sick, and 看病 is to see a doctor (literally to look at the illness), which you do at the 医院.

The Weather

天气怎么样？

今天天气很好。
Jīntiān tiānqì hěn hǎo.
The weather is good today.
今天有雨。
Jīntiān yǒu yǔ.
There is rain today.
天气不好，我在家休息。
Tiānqì bù hǎo, wǒ zài jiā xiūxi.
The weather is bad, so I rest at home.

天气 is the weather and 雨 is rain. 怎么样 from lesson 2 asks how something is.

Putting It Together

A day of food and weather.

我喜欢吃中国菜。
Wǒ xǐhuan chī Zhōngguó cài.
I like to eat Chinese food.
早饭我吃鸡蛋和面包。
Zǎofàn wǒ chī jīdàn hé miànbāo.
For breakfast I eat eggs and bread.
今天我生病了，没去上课。
Wǒ jīntiān shēngbìng le, méi qù shàngkè.
I got sick today, so I did not go to class.
下午天气很好，我和朋友喝茶。
Xiàwǔ tiānqì hěn hǎo, wǒ hé péngyou hē chá.
The weather is nice in the afternoon, so my friend and I drink tea.
