Rain, Snow, and the Road

下雨, 坐车.

今天下雨，我坐出租车。
Jīntiān xià yǔ, wǒ zuò chūzūchē.
It is raining today, so I take a taxi.
下雪了，很冷。
Xià xuě le, hěn lěng.
It is snowing, and it is very cold.
我坐飞机去中国。
Wǒ zuò fēijī qù Zhōngguó.
I take a plane to China.

坐 works for 车, 出租车, 火车, and 飞机. 下雨 and 下雪 both use 下.

Phones and Screens

手机, 电脑, 电视.

我有一个新手机。
Wǒ yǒu yí gè xīn shǒujī.
I have a new mobile phone.
我看电视，也看电影。
Wǒ kàn diànshì, yě kàn diànyǐng.
I watch TV, and I also watch movies.
我给朋友打电话。
Wǒ gěi péngyou dǎ diànhuà.
I call my friend.

Many of these start with 电 (electric): 电话, 电脑, 电视, 电影. 手机 is the hand-machine, your mobile.

Learning Chinese

汉语, 汉字, 中文.

我学汉语，写汉字。
Wǒ xué Hànyǔ, xiě Hànzì.
I study Chinese and write characters.
这本书有很多汉字。
Zhè běn shū yǒu hěn duō Hànzì.
This book has many characters.
中文课很好。
Zhōngwén kè hěn hǎo.
The Chinese class is great.

汉语 is spoken Chinese, 汉字 are the written characters, and 中文 is the language in general.

Things and Pets

桌子, 椅子, 狗, 猫.

桌子上有一个杯子。
Zhuōzi shàng yǒu yí gè bēizi.
There is a cup on the table.
椅子在桌子前。
Yǐzi zài zhuōzi qián.
The chair is in front of the table.
我家有一只狗和一只猫。
Wǒ jiā yǒu yì zhī gǒu hé yì zhī māo.
My family has a dog and a cat.

杯子, 椅子, and 桌子 all end in 子, a common noun ending. Animals take the measure word 只: 一只狗.

Putting It Together

An everyday day.

今天下雨，我在家看电视。
Jīntiān xià yǔ, wǒ zài jiā kàn diànshì.
It is raining today, so I watch TV at home.
我看手机，给朋友打电话。
Wǒ kàn shǒujī, gěi péngyou dǎ diànhuà.
I look at my phone and call my friend.
我喜欢学中文，写汉字。
Wǒ xǐhuan xué Zhōngwén, xiě Hànzì.
I like to study Chinese and write characters.
我家有狗和猫，它们都很好。
Wǒ jiā yǒu gǒu hé māo, tāmen dōu hěn hǎo.
My family has a dog and a cat, and they are both lovely.
