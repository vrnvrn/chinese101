Adjectives with 很

Link with 很, not 是.

今天很冷。
Jīntiān hěn lěng.
Today is cold.
我很忙。
Wǒ hěn máng.
I am busy.
她很高兴，也很漂亮。
Tā hěn gāoxìng, yě hěn piàoliang.
She is happy and also pretty.

Adjectives link to the subject with 很, the way you saw last week. 便宜 cheap, 冷 cold, 热 hot, 忙 busy, 高兴 happy, 漂亮 pretty.

The 好 Adjectives

好 plus a verb.

好 + verb = nice to ...
这个很好吃。
Zhège hěn hǎochī.
This is tasty.
这个很好看，那个很好听。
Zhège hěn hǎokàn, nàge hěn hǎotīng.
This one looks nice, that one sounds nice.
学中文很好玩儿。
Xué Zhōngwén hěn hǎowánr.
Studying Chinese is fun.

好 plus a verb makes an adjective: 好吃 good to eat, 好看 good to look at, 好听 good to hear, 好玩儿 fun.

Yes, No, All, Also

对, 不, 都, 也.

你忙吗？我不忙。
Nǐ máng ma? Wǒ bù máng.
Are you busy? I am not busy.
对，我是学生。
Duì, wǒ shì xuésheng.
Right, I am a student.
我也是学生，我们都是学生。
Wǒ yě shì xuésheng, wǒmen dōu shì xuésheng.
I am also a student, we are all students.

不 makes a sentence negative, 对 means correct. 也 means also and 都 means all; both go right before the verb.

How Much

很, 太, 真, 非常.

这个太贵了。
Zhège tài guì le.
This is too expensive.
中国菜真好吃！
Zhōngguó cài zhēn hǎochī!
Chinese food is really tasty!
我非常高兴。
Wǒ fēicháng gāoxìng.
I am extremely happy.

A scale of degree: 很 is a neutral link, 太 is too much (often with 了), 真 is really, 非常 is extremely.

Happening and Done

正在, 了, 的, 吗, 呢.

我正在学习。
Wǒ zhèngzài xuéxí.
I am studying right now.
我吃了，你呢？
Wǒ chī le, nǐ ne?
I have eaten, and you?
这是我的书。你好吗？
Zhè shì wǒ de shū. Nǐ hǎo ma?
This is my book. How are you?

正在 marks something happening now, 了 marks it done, 的 shows possession, and 吗 and 呢 turn a statement into a question.
