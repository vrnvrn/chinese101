June 23:
HSK 1 Sentence Structures

Every sentence pattern on the HSK 1 (3.0) exam, grouped by theme. This is the grammar half of the test, the part vocabulary alone cannot carry. Read the Chinese, then flip Pinyin and English on only to self-check.

How to use this lesson

By the end of this lesson…

  • 1Build the three core sentence frames: 是, an adjective with 很, and 有
  • 2Ask any question: 吗, the question words, A不A, and 呢
  • 3Say no correctly with 不 and 没(有)
  • 4Order a sentence: subject, time, place, verb, object
  • 5Use the modal verbs 会, 能, 可以, 想, 要 and the particles 的, 了, 吗, 呢, 吧
  • 6Handle numbers, measure words, money, time, and location
  • 7Keep Pinyin and English off, and flip them on only to check yourself

Bolded characters are the structure word doing the work in each pattern. The English is plain and literal so the grammar shows through.

Theme 1, The Core Frames

Three ways to make a sentence.

Almost every HSK 1 statement is one of these three. Learn the frame and you can drop any vocabulary into it.

Noun + 是 + Noun (identity)
我是学生。
Wǒ shì xuéshēng.
I am a student.
Noun + 很 + Adjective (description)
今天很热。
Jīntiān hěn rè.
Today is hot.
Noun + 有 + Noun (have / exist)
我有一个哥哥。
Wǒ yǒu yí gè gēge.
I have an older brother.

Never put 是 in front of an adjective. 我是高兴 is wrong; use 我很高兴. The 很 here is a neutral link, not really very.

Theme 2, Yes or No Questions

Four ways to ask yes or no.

Statement + 吗 ?
你是学生吗？
Nǐ shì xuéshēng ma?
Are you a student?
Verb + 不 + Verb ?
你是不是中国人？
Nǐ shì bu shì Zhōngguó rén?
Are you Chinese (or not)?
Adjective + 不 + Adjective ?
今天冷不冷？
Jīntiān lěng bu lěng?
Is it cold today?
Statement + 好吗 / 对吗 ?
我们去吃饭，好吗？
Wǒmen qù chīfàn, hǎo ma?
Let us go eat, okay?
Theme 3, Information Questions

Question words sit where the answer goes.

Mandarin keeps statement word order. Drop the question word into the exact slot the answer would fill. No flipping the sentence around.

什么 (what)
你叫什么名字？
Nǐ jiào shénme míngzi?
What is your name?
谁 (who)
他是谁？
Tā shì shéi?
Who is he?
哪儿 / 哪里 (where)
你在哪儿？
Nǐ zài nǎr?
Where are you?
几 / 多少 (how many, how much)
你家有几口人？这个多少钱？
Nǐ jiā yǒu jǐ kǒu rén? Zhège duōshao qián?
How many people are in your family? How much is this?
什么时候 / 怎么 / 怎么样 / 为什么
你什么时候来？今天天气怎么样？
Nǐ shénme shíhou lái? Jīntiān tiānqì zěnmeyàng?
When are you coming? How is the weather today?

几 expects a small number and needs a measure word (几个). 多少 works for any amount and usually drops the measure word.

Theme 4, Saying No

不 for now, 没 for the past.

不 + verb / adjective (present, future, habit)
我不喝茶。
Wǒ bù hē chá.
I do not drink tea.
没(有) + verb (it did not happen)
我昨天没去。
Wǒ zuótiān méi qù.
I did not go yesterday.
没有 is the only way to negate 有
他没有手机。
Tā méiyǒu shǒujī.
He does not have a phone.

Quick test: if you can say it with 有, negate with 没. For everything else in the present, use 不. Past actions that did not happen always take 没.

Theme 5, Word Order

Subject, time, place, verb, object.

Time and place go before the verb in Chinese, the opposite of English. This single rule fixes most sentence errors.

Subject + Time + Verb + Object
我晚上看电视。
Wǒ wǎnshang kàn diànshì.
I watch TV in the evening.
Subject + 在 + Place + Verb + Object
他在学校学习。
Tā zài xuéxiào xuéxí.
He studies at school.
Subject + Time + Place + Verb (full chain)
我明天在家吃饭。
Wǒ míngtiān zài jiā chīfàn.
Tomorrow I will eat at home.
Movement verbs take the place directly
我去北京，他回家。
Wǒ qù Běijīng, tā huí jiā.
I go to Beijing, he goes home.
Theme 6, Wanting and Being Able

Modal verbs before the main verb.

会 (a learned skill)
我会说汉语。
Wǒ huì shuō Hànyǔ.
I can speak Chinese (I learned how).
能 (able, free, allowed by circumstance)
你能来吗？
Nǐ néng lái ma?
Can you come?
可以 (permitted to)
我可以坐这儿吗？
Wǒ kěyǐ zuò zhèr ma?
May I sit here?
想 (would like to) / 要 (want, will)
我想吃饺子，我要一杯水。
Wǒ xiǎng chī jiǎozi, wǒ yào yì bēi shuǐ.
I would like to eat dumplings, I want a glass of water.
喜欢 / 觉得 + verb or clause
我喜欢看书，我觉得很好。
Wǒ xǐhuan kàn shū, wǒ juéde hěn hǎo.
I like reading, I think it is great.
Theme 7, Describing with Degree

很, 太…了, 真, 非常, 不太.

很 + adjective (neutral)
这本书很好看。
Zhè běn shū hěn hǎokàn.
This book is good.
太 + adjective + 了 (too much)
这个手机太贵了！
Zhège shǒujī tài guì le!
This phone is too expensive!
真 (really) / 非常 (extremely)
中国菜真好吃，我非常高兴。
Zhōngguó cài zhēn hǎochī, wǒ fēicháng gāoxìng.
Chinese food is really tasty, I am extremely happy.
不太 (not very) / 有点儿 (a bit too)
今天不太冷，但是有点儿累。
Jīntiān bú tài lěng, dànshì yǒudiǎnr lèi.
Today is not very cold, but I am a bit tired.

有点儿 goes before the adjective and carries a complaint (一点 too much). 一点儿 goes after the verb or adjective for a small amount (喝一点儿 = drink a little).

Theme 8, The Little Words

Particles: 的, 了, 吗, 呢, 吧.

的 (possessive and modifier)
这是我的书，那是漂亮的衣服。
Zhè shì wǒ de shū, nà shì piàoliang de yīfu.
This is my book, that is pretty clothing.
了 after the verb (completed action)
我吃了饭。
Wǒ chī le fàn.
I have eaten.
了 at the end (a change happened)
下雨了，天气冷了。
Xià yǔ le, tiānqì lěng le.
It started raining, the weather has turned cold.
呢 (and you?) / 吧 (suggestion or guess)
我很好，你呢？我们走吧。
Wǒ hěn hǎo, nǐ ne? Wǒmen zǒu ba.
I am fine, and you? Let us go.
Theme 9, Numbers and Measure Words

Counting things, money, and time.

Number + Measure Word + Noun
我有两个哥哥，三本书。
Wǒ yǒu liǎng gè gēge, sān běn shū.
I have two older brothers and three books.
两 before a measure word, 二 for counting
现在二点，我要两杯茶。
Xiànzài èr diǎn, wǒ yào liǎng bēi chá.
It is two o'clock, I want two cups of tea.
Price: number + 块 (钱)
这本书五十块钱。
Zhè běn shū wǔshí kuài qián.
This book is fifty yuan.
Clock: 点 for hours, 分 for minutes, 半 for half
现在五点半，我七点起床。
Xiànzài wǔ diǎn bàn, wǒ qī diǎn qǐchuáng.
It is five thirty now, I get up at seven.
Date: big to small, 年 月 日
今天是六月二十三号。
Jīntiān shì liù yuè èrshísān hào.
Today is June 23.
Theme 10, Where Things Are

在, 有, and position words.

Thing + 在 + Place (location of a thing)
书在桌子上。
Shū zài zhuōzi shàng.
The book is on the table.
Place + 有 + Thing (what exists there)
学校里有一个书店。
Xuéxiào lǐ yǒu yí gè shūdiàn.
There is a bookstore in the school.
Noun + position word (上, 下, 里, 前, 后, 旁边)
猫在椅子下，他在我旁边。
Māo zài yǐzi xià, tā zài wǒ pángbiān.
The cat is under the chair, he is next to me.
这儿 / 那儿 (here, there)
你来这儿，他在那儿。
Nǐ lái zhèr, tā zài nàr.
Come here, he is over there.
Theme 11, Connectors and Prepositions

和, 给, 跟, 也, 都.

和 joins two nouns
我和爸爸去学校。
Wǒ hé bàba qù xuéxiào.
My dad and I go to school.
给 + person + verb (do something for someone)
我给你打电话。
Wǒ gěi nǐ dǎ diànhuà.
I will call you.
跟 + person + verb (do something with someone)
我跟朋友看电影。
Wǒ gēn péngyou kàn diànyǐng.
I watch a movie with a friend.
也 (also) and 都 (all) sit before the verb
我们都是学生，他也很忙。
Wǒmen dōu shì xuéshēng, tā yě hěn máng.
We are all students, he is also very busy.

和 connects nouns only, never verbs or whole sentences. To join actions, just say them in order: 我吃饭，看电视.

Theme 12, Right Now and Just Happened

正在 for ongoing, 了 for done.

(正)在 + verb (+ 呢) for an action in progress
他正在睡觉呢。
Tā zhèngzài shuìjiào ne.
He is sleeping right now.
verb + 了 for a completed action
我买了一个手机。
Wǒ mǎi le yí gè shǒujī.
I bought a phone.
没 cancels the 了 (it did not happen)
我没买手机。
Wǒ méi mǎi shǒujī.
I did not buy a phone.

Once you add 没, drop the 了. You say 我没去, never 我没去了.

Theme 13, Everyday Set Patterns

Phrases that come up on every exam.

请 + verb (polite request)
请坐，请问，您叫什么名字？
Qǐng zuò, qǐngwèn, nín jiào shénme míngzi?
Please sit. Excuse me, what is your name?
Separable verbs (动词 split apart)
我睡了八个小时觉。
Wǒ shuì le bā gè xiǎoshí jiào.
I slept for eight hours.
好 + verb (good to do)
这个菜很好吃，这首歌很好听。
Zhège cài hěn hǎochī, zhè shǒu gē hěn hǎotīng.
This dish is tasty, this song sounds great.
还是 (or) in a question
你喝茶还是喝水？
Nǐ hē chá háishì hē shuǐ?
Do you drink tea or water?
一边 A 一边 B is beyond HSK 1, but 一点儿 / 一下 are core
请等一下，我会一点儿汉语。
Qǐng děng yíxià, wǒ huì yìdiǎnr Hànyǔ.
Please wait a moment, I know a little Chinese.
You have the whole grammar map

Thirteen themes, one test.

Every sentence on HSK 1 (3.0) is built from these patterns. The reading and listening sections just recombine them with the 300 words. If you can read each Chinese line above and explain why it is built that way, the grammar will not surprise you.
