June 24:
HSK 1 Exam Cram

The single highest-yield review before the test. The words that show up on every page, the sentence patterns that carry the most points, and the four traps that cost the most marks. If your time is short, this is the page to drill. Read the Chinese first; flip Pinyin and English on only to self-check.

How to use this lesson

By the end of this lesson…

  • 1Recognize the forty function words and verbs that appear most on the exam
  • 2Produce the three core frames on demand: 是, 很 + adjective, and 有
  • 3Ask and answer every question type with the right word
  • 4Avoid the four traps: 不 vs 没, 是 vs 很, word order, and 两 vs 二
  • 5Read ten ready-made survival sentences out loud without hesitating
  • 6Walk into the exam with a one-page mental checklist

This lesson assumes you already met the 300 words and the thirteen sentence themes. It is the compression, not the introduction. Bolded characters are the word doing the work.

Part 2, The Three Core Frames

Every statement is one of three.

If you can build these three frames automatically, you can answer most of the test. Drill them until you do not have to think.

Noun + 是 + Noun (identity)
我是中国人。
Wǒ shì Zhōngguó rén.
I am Chinese.
Noun + 很 + Adjective (description, no 是)
这个菜很好吃。
Zhège cài hěn hǎochī.
This dish is tasty.
Noun + 有 + Noun (have, exist)
我有两个孩子。
Wǒ yǒu liǎng gè háizi.
I have two children.

Negate 是 and 有 the right way: 我不是老师, 我没有钱. The adjective frame negates with 不: 不太好.

Part 3, Questions in One Glance

The question word sits where the answer goes.

Statement + 吗 (yes or no)
你是学生吗？
Nǐ shì xuéshēng ma?
Are you a student?
什么 / 谁 / 哪儿 (what, who, where)
你叫什么名字？你去哪儿？
Nǐ jiào shénme míngzi? Nǐ qù nǎr?
What is your name? Where are you going?
几 / 多少 (how many, how much)
现在几点？这个多少钱？
Xiànzài jǐ diǎn? Zhège duōshao qián?
What time is it? How much is this?
怎么样 / 为什么 / 什么时候 (how, why, when)
今天天气怎么样？你什么时候来？
Jīntiān tiānqì zěnmeyàng? Nǐ shénme shíhou lái?
How is the weather today? When are you coming?

Do not flip the sentence around like English. Keep statement order and drop the question word into the exact slot the answer fills.

Part 4, The Four Traps

Where the points leak out.

Most lost marks come from these four. Burn them in tonight.

Trap 1: 不 (present, future, habit) vs 没 (it did not happen)
我不喝咖啡。我昨天没去。
Wǒ bù hē kāfēi. Wǒ zuótiān méi qù.
I do not drink coffee. I did not go yesterday.
Trap 2: never put 是 before an adjective
我很高兴。（我是高兴 ✗）
Wǒ hěn gāoxìng.
I am happy.
Trap 3: time and place go before the verb
我明天在家吃饭。（吃饭在家 ✗）
Wǒ míngtiān zài jiā chīfàn.
Tomorrow I will eat at home.
Trap 4: 两 before a measure word, 二 for counting
我要两个，现在二点。
Wǒ yào liǎng gè, xiànzài èr diǎn.
I want two, it is two o'clock.
If you only remember one thing: 没 cancels 了. Say 我没去, never 我没去了.
Part 5, Ten Survival Sentences

Say these without thinking.

Ready-made sentences that cover greetings, requests, and the most common exam prompts. Read each one out loud until it is automatic.

你好，请问，您贵姓？
Nǐ hǎo, qǐngwèn, nín guìxìng?
Hello, may I ask your surname?
我叫李明，我是美国人。
Wǒ jiào Lǐ Míng, wǒ shì Měiguó rén.
My name is Li Ming, I am American.
我会说一点儿汉语。
Wǒ huì shuō yìdiǎnr Hànyǔ.
I can speak a little Chinese.
这个多少钱？太贵了。
Zhège duōshao qián? Tài guì le.
How much is this? Too expensive.
我想喝水，请给我一杯水。
Wǒ xiǎng hē shuǐ, qǐng gěi wǒ yì bēi shuǐ.
I would like water, please give me a glass.
今天是六月二十四号，星期三。
Jīntiān shì liù yuè èrshísì hào, xīngqīsān.
Today is June 24, Wednesday.
对不起，我听不懂，请再说一遍。
Duìbuqǐ, wǒ tīng bu dǒng, qǐng zài shuō yí biàn.
Sorry, I do not understand, please say it again.
Exam day checklist

One page in your head.

Three frames (是, 很, 有). Question word goes in the answer slot. 不 for now, 没 for the past, and 没 kills 了. Time and place before the verb. 两 with a measure word. If you carry just this in, the rest is recombining the 300 words you already know.

Final drill: cover the Pinyin and English, scroll back to Part 1, and read every word and sentence out loud. Anything you stumble on is tonight's study list.
