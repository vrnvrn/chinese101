Describing Things

很, 太, 真, 非常.

这个很便宜，那个太贵。
Zhège hěn piányi, nàge tài guì.
This one is cheap, that one is too expensive.
今天很热，昨天很冷。
Jīntiān hěn rè, zuótiān hěn lěng.
Today is hot, yesterday was cold.
中国菜真好吃！
Zhōngguó cài zhēn hǎochī!
Chinese food is really tasty!
我非常高兴，也很忙。
Wǒ fēicháng gāoxìng, yě hěn máng.
I am extremely happy, and also very busy.

很 is a neutral link, 太 means too much, 真 means really, and 非常 means extremely.

Places and Meals

Out for a meal.

我去饭店吃晚饭。
Wǒ qù fàndiàn chī wǎnfàn.
I go to the restaurant for dinner.
超市里的水果很便宜。
Chāoshì lǐ de shuǐguǒ hěn piányi.
The fruit in the supermarket is cheap.
早上我在家吃早饭，喝牛奶。
Zǎoshang wǒ zài jiā chī zǎofàn, hē niúnǎi.
In the morning I eat breakfast and drink milk at home.
学校里有一个书店。
Xuéxiào lǐ yǒu yí gè shūdiàn.
There is a bookstore in the school.
Weather and Getting Around

A rainy day.

今天下雨，我坐出租车去公司。
Jīntiān xià yǔ, wǒ zuò chūzūchē qù gōngsī.
It is raining today, so I take a taxi to the company.
下雪了，很冷，我在家看电影。
Xià xuě le, hěn lěng, wǒ zài jiā kàn diànyǐng.
It is snowing and cold, so I watch a movie at home.
天气很好的时候，我和朋友去玩。
Tiānqì hěn hǎo de shíhou, wǒ hé péngyou qù wán.
When the weather is nice, my friend and I go out.
我坐火车去看家人。
Wǒ zuò huǒchē qù kàn jiārén.
I take the train to visit my family.
Tech and Study

Phones, screens, and Chinese.

我有手机和电脑。
Wǒ yǒu shǒujī hé diànnǎo.
I have a mobile phone and a computer.
晚上我看电视，听歌。
Wǎnshang wǒ kàn diànshì, tīng gē.
In the evening I watch TV and listen to songs.
我学中文，写汉字，觉得很好。
Wǒ xué Zhōngwén, xiě Hànzì, juéde hěn hǎo.
I study Chinese and write characters, and I think it is great.
这本书很好看，也很便宜。
Zhè běn shū hěn hǎokàn, yě hěn piányi.
This book is good and also cheap.
Dialogue, At a Restaurant

Ordering a meal.

A
你好！我要米饭和饺子。
Nǐ hǎo! Wǒ yào mǐfàn hé jiǎozi.
Hello! I would like rice and dumplings.
B
好。你喝什么？
Hǎo. Nǐ hē shénme?
Sure. What would you like to drink?
A
我要茶，谢谢。
Wǒ yào chá, xièxie.
I would like tea, thank you.
B
好的！
Hǎo de!
All right!
Dialogue, Talking About the Weather

What shall we do?

A
今天天气怎么样？
Jīntiān tiānqì zěnmeyàng?
How is the weather today?
B
今天下雨，很冷。
Jīntiān xià yǔ, hěn lěng.
It is raining today, and cold.
A
那我们在家看电影吧。
Nà wǒmen zài jiā kàn diànyǐng ba.
Then let us watch a movie at home.
B
好！我很喜欢看电影。
Hǎo! Wǒ hěn xǐhuan kàn diànyǐng.
Great! I really like watching movies.
Dialogue, At the Shop

How much is it?

A
你好，这个手机多少钱？
Nǐ hǎo, zhège shǒujī duōshao qián?
Hello, how much is this phone?
B
这个手机太贵了。那个便宜一点儿。
Zhège shǒujī tài guì le. Nàge piányi yìdiǎnr.
This phone is too expensive. That one is a bit cheaper.
A
我要那个，谢谢！
Wǒ yào nàge, xièxie!
I will take that one, thank you!
